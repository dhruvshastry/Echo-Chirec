Our Mission

The Echo is a completely student-created and led e-newspaper where students voice their opinions, share ideas, and gain the exposure needed to nurture their talents.

We bring stories of the student body and the stories that matter to our community. Journalism, graphic design, and photography — explored by CHIRECians, for CHIRECians.
