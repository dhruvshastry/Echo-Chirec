Photojournalism

Moments captured by The Echo’s student photographers — light, place, and perspective.

November 2023
🛕

Marveling at the serenity of the giant Buddha statue at Chin Swee Caves Temple, where ancient wisdom meets modern awe. A symbol of peace that stands tall amidst the beauty of nature.

— Shrinithi Kathiresan Subramani
🌺

The brilliant, blood red Calliandra stands out against the bright green leaves in the pleasant sunlight. Unusually beautiful spikes spread out as petals with dainty, dew-drop-like white spots adorning the ends of each spike.

— Abhimanyu Mehta
🌆

The sun begins its descent, painting the sky with a mesmerising blend of oranges and pinks. Standing on the bridge, the urban landscape is transformed into a tapestry of warm colors, with a train passing beneath.

— Namita Sai Vegesna
🌊

Mumbai, the city of dreams. The sea emits an aura of serenity while the run-down boats show the humbling truth of the city. In the background, the India Gate stands tall — anchoring the heritage rooted in the past.

— Arghodeep Banerjee
🕯️

A stunning sanctuary in Malaysia called Chin Cave Temple captures the atmosphere of serenity and spiritual reverence — a tribute to ageless tradition and natural beauty.

— Shrinithi Kathiresan Subramani
🐨

The koala, with its wide round head, thick furry ears, and big black nose, sleeps for up to 18 hours a day, eats eucalyptus leaves, and basically hangs out.

— Namita Sai Vegesna
🚗

In the heart of Mumbai — a dance of chaos and serenity under the azure sky. Colourful cars and people draped in tradition merge to create the city’s soul.

— Arghodeep Banerjee
December 2022
⛰️

Mountains feel alien — not because they are weird, but because their beauty can never really be captured by our cameras, only by the eyes we hold on our heads.

— Nia Vadapalli
🍃

A photograph clicked while taking a stroll in a park — serenity and tranquility. The free spirit of the leaves makes it feel as if they’re growing like they are a part of the sky.

— Rhea Masih
🗼

The infamous Space Needle in Seattle. A cloudy morning made better by the view of the icon of Washington and its exquisite architecture.

— Manaswini Kanakala
🌅

The horizon was breathtaking. The sky merged with the water, separated only by the golden lights that shone brightly despite the darkness all around.

— Saanvi Hora
💧

The Rock Garden waterfall in the hills of Darjeeling, West Bengal. The frothy water has an aura of peace and prosperity as it rushes down the rocks.

— Adithya Chandrashekar
✨

“Hold fast to these dreams, for if dreams die, life is a broken-winged bird that cannot fly.”

— Saakshi Sonti
🌸

Beauty is present everywhere in our daily travels. I see it in sunsets, hear it in the chirping of birds, and smell it in the aroma of food.

— Tanvi Reddy
